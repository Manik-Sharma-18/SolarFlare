\section*{Abstract}

This thesis develops a self-supervised pretraining pipeline for
solar flare forecasting based on the joint-embedding predictive
architecture (JEPA) family, in particular the \emph{V-JEPA-2-AC}
template of Assran~et~al.\ (2024). The training corpus comprises twenty-one active-
region cubes of magnetic winding flux at twelve-minute cadence,
two orders of magnitude smaller than the smallest comparable
video self-supervised regime. The model uses a ViT-Small context
encoder with two-dimensional rotary positional embeddings in
megametre units, an exponential-moving-average target encoder,
and a six-layer block-causal predictor; training is performed by
smooth-$L_{1}$ regression in the embedding space, restricted by
construction to the intersection of masked, data-valid, and
pad-valid tokens.

Four ablation families fix the operating point. The mask-policy
ablation establishes that a uniform mixture over the five
mask families (short tube, long tube, future block, cross-time,
tail) yields a sanity-scale floor of $0.00530$ smooth-$L_{1}$,
$3.4 \times$ better than a tube-only variant and $1.6 \times$
better than the previous tube-heavy default. The EMA-decay sweep
identifies $\tau = 0.990$ as the winner over the tested range
$\{0.990, 0.994, 0.998, 0.9995\}$, with a monotone response in
which a smaller $\tau$ yields lower validation loss. The mask-ratio sweep identifies $r = 0.75$ as
the winner over $\{0.60, 0.75, 0.85, 0.90\}$, with a concave
response curve. A thesis-scale run on thirteen training cubes for one hundred
epochs of two thousand steps fixes the final operating point at
a $5.8 \times$ throughput improvement over the earlier
sanity-scale configuration.

A frozen-feature winding-flux probe attached to the encoder
regresses the spatial-mean winding-flux curve $\langle W \rangle(t)$,
the literature-supported flare precursor of
Raphaldini~et~al.\ (2022). On the encoder-validation cubes the
multilayer-perceptron probe reaches $R^{2} = 0.73$, Pearson
$r = 0.87$, and median absolute percentage error (medAPE) $7.3\%$.
On five novel held-out active regions, the per-cube Pearson
correlation is $0.7$--$0.9$ and a per-cube two-parameter log-affine
recalibration (one $(a, b)$ pair fitted on a single window per
cube) recovers a median medAPE of $9.9\%$, beating the lag-one
persistence baseline on four of the five novel cubes. A secondary
frozen-feature flare classifier delivers AUC~$=1.000$ and
TSS~$=1.000$ on harp\_49 at the C-class-or-higher / twelve-hour /
MLP configuration, exceeding the persistence floor of $0.975$ on
the densest mixed-label evaluation cube.

The four explicit gaps separating the present pretraining result
from an operational flare forecaster --- corpus scale, downstream
head, persistence-anchored skill metrics, and multimodal SHARP
fusion --- are documented as future work.
