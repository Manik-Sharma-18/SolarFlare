\caption[Spatial power spectrum]{\textbf{Winding power persists to the smallest
spatial scales.} Radially-averaged spatial power spectrum $P(k)$
(Eq.~\eqref{eq:psd}) of the asinh winding field, averaged over active regions and
normalised to the largest scale. The horizontal axis is the spatial wavenumber
$k=2\pi/\lambda$: small $k$ (left) is the large-scale envelope, large $k$ (right)
is pixel-scale detail. Power falls off only gradually with $k$ and never reaches a
flat noise floor: there is real structure at every scale. Combined with the temporal incoherence of
those small scales (Figure~\ref{fig:autocorr}), this justifies removing the
fine-scale band (shaded) by low-pass before forecasting.}
