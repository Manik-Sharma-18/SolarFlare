\chapter{Hyperparameters and Optimiser Settings}
\label{app:hyperparams}

This appendix records the hyperparameter sets used for the four
load-bearing runs of Chapter~\ref{ch:experiments}. Settings not
listed are inherited from the V-JEPA-2-AC defaults
of~\cite{vjepa2_2025}; differences from these defaults are flagged
explicitly.

\label{app:opt}

\centering

\midrule

$\beta_{1}, \beta_{2}$      & $0.9, 0.95$ \\
