    1. SelfAttentionMemory(nn.Module):

         - Gate: combined = cat([z_h, z_m], dim=1), gate = sigmoid(self.gate(combined))
         - z_fused = gate * z_h + (1-gate) * z_m
         - m_new = z_fused (memory update)
         - h_out = h + output_proj(z_fused) (residual connection)
         - return h_out, m_new
